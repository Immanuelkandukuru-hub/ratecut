❓ How It Works

RateCut helps you in 3 simple steps:

1

Enter Your Budget

Tell us how much money you want to spend (e.g. ₹500, ₹1,000 or ₹2,000). Choose your household size so we can help you plan better.

2

Add Products & Compare Prices

Add essential products like rice, dal, oil and vegetables to your list. Then compare prices from different shops to find the best deal.

3

Choose & Save

Pick the basket with the lowest cost. See your potential savings and save your shopping list to review later.
